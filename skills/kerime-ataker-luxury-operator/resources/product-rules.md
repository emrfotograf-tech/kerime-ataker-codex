# KERIME ATAKER — PRODUCT RULES

## CORE PRODUCT WORLDS

Primary worlds:
- leather
- sculptural womenswear
- refined eveningwear
- silk
- satin
- fur / outerwear
- selective bridal

Lingerie is not the main brand positioning.
Use lingerie only when context fits:
- bridal
- honeymoon
- trousseau
- private capsule
- selective intimate edit

Do not use lingerie as default for public emotional campaigns such as Mother’s Day or teacher campaigns.

## PRODUCT ANALYSIS REQUIREMENTS

Every product must be analyzed through:

1. Product character
2. Material language
3. Silhouette logic
4. Emotional tone
5. Seasonal fit
6. Occasion fit
7. Boutique fit
8. U.S. city fit
9. Instagram role
10. Meta ads role

## PRODUCT LOCK

Never invent product names.
Never invent product details.
If product link or image is unclear, mark as:
requires verification.

## POSITIONING RULE

Each product must answer:

- Is this product strengthening Kerime Ataker as elevated sensual luxury womenswear?
- Does it feel boutique-compatible?
- Does it avoid cheap or overly commercial language?
- Does it support Instagram profile movement?
