# KERIME ATAKER — INSTAGRAM OUTPUT SYSTEM

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Instagram is not a direct sales platform.
Instagram is:
- luxury positioning
- emotional branding
- editorial storytelling
- product aura building

---

# CAPTION SYSTEM

Captions must:
- feel cinematic
- feel emotionally restrained
- feel premium
- avoid over-explaining
- avoid hard selling

Structure priority:
1. Emotional atmosphere
2. Material feeling
3. Silhouette / movement
4. Soft direction

Never:
- aggressively push purchase
- sound mass-market
- use influencer language
- use cheap CTA

---

# STORY SYSTEM

Stories should:
- feel immediate
- feel elegant
- feel visually led
- use minimal text

Preferred story feeling:
- editorial note
- whispered luxury
- cinematic fragment
- material detail

Story text must:
- stay short
- stay clean
- never overcrowd visuals

---

# REELS SYSTEM

Reels must:
- prioritize movement
- prioritize atmosphere
- prioritize material behavior
- prioritize silhouette energy

Reels text must:
- remain minimal
- never dominate the frame
- support the visual rhythm

Preferred structure:
0-3 sec:
- hook atmosphere

4-7 sec:
- texture / movement

8-12 sec:
- silhouette / emotional build

13-15 sec:
- soft direction or product presence

---

# HASHTAG SYSTEM

Hashtags must:
- stay selective
- stay premium
- stay relevant

Avoid:
- spam hashtags
- generic viral hashtags
- mass-fashion hashtags

Preferred count:
- 5 to 8 maximum

---

# KERIME ATAKER LANGUAGE LOCK

The brand voice must always feel:
- refined
- intelligent
- sensual but controlled
- editorial
- emotionally disciplined
- luxurious without exaggeration

Never:
- sound desperate
- sound cheap
- sound trend-hungry
- sound algorithm-chasing
