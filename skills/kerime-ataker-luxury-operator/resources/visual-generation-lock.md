# KERIME ATAKER — VISUAL GENERATION LOCK

## CORE PRINCIPLE

All visual outputs must feel like:
- real photography
- luxury editorial campaigns
- authentic camera capture
- natural cinematic lighting

Never feel like:
- CGI
- 3D render
- plastic AI image
- fake beauty render
- game-engine output

---

# PRODUCT PRESERVATION LOCK

Products must preserve:

- stitching
- seams
- zipper structure
- buttons
- leather texture
- fur density
- fabric behavior
- silhouette integrity
- tailoring proportions
- hardware geometry

Never:
- redesign products
- simplify garments
- generate fake stitching
- distort tailoring
- hallucinate materials

---

# FACE & BODY LOCK

Models must preserve:

- identity
- facial structure
- skin texture
- natural anatomy
- realistic body movement

Avoid:
- AI beauty-face
- distorted anatomy
- fake skin
- plastic skin
- over-symmetry
- unrealistic limbs

---

# LIGHTING LOCK

Lighting must preserve:
- real shadow behavior
- natural highlight behavior
- authentic depth
- realistic exposure
- real camera feeling

Avoid:
- flat lighting
- over-HDR
- fake glow
- artificial skin shine
- homogeneous light
